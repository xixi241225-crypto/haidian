03 / WELLBEING

From Infrastructure Severance to Wellbeing Connections

Railways, ring roads, waterways, compounds, and large blocks increase walking time and opportunity costs. A low-tech baseline repairs access, rest, shade, help, and choice before cautious AI enhancement.

Low-Tech Baseline

Fixed wayfinding, printed information, staffed service, ordinary checkout, continuous seating, and basic accessibility remain available.

+
AI Enhancement

AI enhances service only with a public purpose, minimum data, human review, interruptibility, and an exit path.

C01 Kendall Square / MIT, USA

MIT’s resource map lists campus mentoring, licensing and entrepreneurship resources alongside off-campus incubation and shared-laboratory resources. This is a directory, not evidence of collaboration outcomes.

  • Institution interface: campus and off-campus resource directory.
  • Shared facilities: LabCentral is listed; access terms were not verified.
  • Talent daily life: mentoring and venture support are listed; wellbeing outcomes are unproven.
  • Scenario access: the source supplies no public-space testing permission.
  • Community participation: representative public involvement is not documented here.
  • Failure and exit: exit agreements are not documented here.

Jing-Zhang proposal: Provide staffed resource interfaces at both AI Origin district gateways, using a shared directory of university services, advice and bookable facilities; providers would maintain access rules and hours.

Do not copy institutional density, financing or access rights; a directory does not establish resource availability in Jing-Zhang.

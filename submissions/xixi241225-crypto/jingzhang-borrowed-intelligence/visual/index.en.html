Three Scales, One Evidence Chain

03 / WELLBEING

From Infrastructure Severance to Wellbeing Connections

Railways, ring roads, waterways, compounds, and large blocks increase walking time and opportunity costs. A low-tech baseline repairs access, rest, shade, help, and choice before cautious AI enhancement.

Low-Tech Baseline

Fixed wayfinding, printed information, staffed service, ordinary checkout, continuous seating, and basic accessibility remain available.

+
AI Enhancement

AI enhances service only with a public purpose, minimum data, human review, interruptibility, and an exit path.

04 / URBAN SPACE

Turning Borrowed Intelligence into Urban Space

The model shifts from single-purpose industrial zoning toward a compound structure of innovative production, daily life, public space, and ecological ground. AI scenarios remain overlays, not invented statutory land-use classes.

Cases, regional interfaces and industry support

This revision replaces the candidate-name index with six cases supported by registered primary sources. They cover AI, robotics and broader innovation districts: the comparison concerns institutions, space and operations, not a claim that all are AI-only parks or that website descriptions prove outcomes. Sourced observations and Jing-Zhang design proposals are separate; undocumented exit, participation and access conditions remain unverified.

C01 Kendall Square / MIT, USA

MIT’s resource map lists campus mentoring, licensing and entrepreneurship resources alongside off-campus incubation and shared-laboratory resources. This is a directory, not evidence of collaboration outcomes.

  • Institution interface: campus and off-campus resource directory.
  • Shared facilities: LabCentral is listed; access terms were not verified.
  • Talent daily life: mentoring and venture support are listed; wellbeing outcomes are unproven.
  • Scenario access: the source supplies no public-space testing permission.
  • Community participation: representative public involvement is not documented here.
  • Failure and exit: exit agreements are not documented here.

Jing-Zhang proposal: Use AI Origin’s two gates as a single resource foyer, with staffed directories of university services, advice and bookable facilities; providers would maintain access rules and hours.

Do not copy institutional density, financing or access rights; a directory does not establish resource availability in Jing-Zhang.

C02 one-north / LaunchPad, Singapore

JTC describes a development bringing together startups, incubators, accelerators and investors, with modular space, amenities and opportunities to pilot across its property portfolio.

  • Institution interface: eligibility is defined by participant type.
  • Shared facilities: modular units; access is not automatically free or unrestricted.
  • Talent daily life: food and transport information accompanies workspace information.
  • Scenario access: portfolio-wide piloting opportunities still require project admission.
  • Community participation: resident co-decision is not established by this page.
  • Failure and exit: flexible leases are described; test-failure rules are not documented.

Jing-Zhang proposal: Provide adaptable small work units and controlled test courts at Zhongzhiyuan, with daily services at AI Origin. The foyer would disclose booking, duration, charges and suspension terms.

Do not transplant Singapore’s rent, subsidy or eligibility regime; verify premises, fire safety, responsibilities and community acceptance locally.

C04 Toronto MaRS, Canada

MaRS combines innovation workspace, meeting and event venues, and commercialization advice, and describes adaptation of a former hospital heritage building for innovation workspace.

  • Institution interface: workspace connects with investor interaction and advice.
  • Shared facilities: meeting and event venues; eligibility and pricing require further checks.
  • Talent daily life: an urban work and meeting setting; universal affordability is not established.
  • Scenario access: this page promises no public testbed.
  • Community participation: resident veto rights are not established here.
  • Failure and exit: service-removal agreements are not documented here.

Jing-Zhang proposal: Use accessible ground floors of existing buildings in the technology-services wing for advice, display and short meetings; retain authentic interpretation at Dazhongsi and make additions removable.

Do not equate adaptation with permission to alter buildings; structure, fire safety, ownership and heritage require review.

C05 London Knowledge Quarter, UK

KQ links academic, cultural, research and technology organisations through a partnership and publishes its governance and knowledge-exchange aims, organising proximity into an exchange network.

  • Institution interface: partnership, board and member representation.
  • Shared facilities: members hold diverse resources; membership creates no universal access right.
  • Talent daily life: exchange network; no living-cost outcome evidence here.
  • Scenario access: knowledge exchange is not permission for real-data tests.
  • Community participation: visitors and local users are included in aims; representation needs verification.
  • Failure and exit: project-stopping thresholds are not documented here.

Jing-Zhang proposal: Create a small partnership forum linking the technology-services wing and spine, with deliberation tables showing institutional resources and community issues. Membership would confer no control over public space.

Partnership discussion cannot replace public procedures; member counts and brand exposure cannot substitute for public outcomes.

  • Institution interface: university, manufacturing research and industry collaboration.
  • Shared facilities: research infrastructure; public-access terms were not verified.
  • Talent daily life: housing and daily convenience are not quantified here.
  • Scenario access: translational research space, without blanket public-testing rights.
  • Community participation: equitable opportunity is an aim; implementation representation is unverified.
